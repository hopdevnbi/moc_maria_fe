# Mộc Maria homepage brand assets

Created with the built-in imagegen tool for this homepage redesign. The photographs are illustrations of the design mood, not photographs of the actual business premises. The visible homepage labels the hero accordingly. The user-provided Facebook screenshot informed the olive green, botanical identity and warm wellness direction. No Facebook photos were copied.

## Files

- `public/brand/moc-maria-mark.webp`: transparent botanical M monogram, optimized from the generated PNG.
- `src/app/icon.png`: favicon derived from the same mark.
- `public/images/wellness-sanctuary.webp`: illustrative interior hero.
- `public/images/wellness-ritual.webp`: illustrative tea and linen still life.

Original generated PNGs are retained in the user's Codex generated_images directory. WebP conversion, transparent padding trim and favicon resize were performed with the project's Sharp dependency.
